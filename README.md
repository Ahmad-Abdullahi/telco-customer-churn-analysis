## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains a comprehensive business analysis of customer attrition (churn) for a fictional telecommunications provider, ABC Communications. The primary objective of this project was to clean, analyze, and visualize customer data to identify the core drivers of churn and provide actionable, data-driven retention strategies to stabilize monthly recurring revenue.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
* **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning via Power Query, data type conversion, and handling missing values.
* **Statistical Analysis:** Generated correlation matrices and descriptive summary statistics.
* **Data Visualization:** Built interactive dashboards utilizing bar charts, histograms, and box-and-whisker plots.
* **Business Intelligence:** Translated raw analytical data into strategic business insights, identifying key organizational risks and opportunities.

## 📊 Key Business Insights
1. **Contract Type Heavily Influences Retention:** Customers on Month-to-Month contracts exhibit the highest churn rates, whereas long-term secured contracts demonstrate strong loyalty.
2. **Premium Product Vulnerability:** The premium Fiber Optic internet service drives disproportionate churn compared to standard DSL, indicating potential service or pricing dissatisfaction.
3. **Immediate Onboarding Drop-off:** Tenure distribution reveals a massive spike in customer attrition within the very first few months of service. 
4. **Price Sensitivity:** Higher monthly charges strongly correlate with a higher likelihood of customer churn.
5. **Payment Friction:** Customers paying via manual "Electronic Check" churn at substantially higher rates than those using automated billing methods.

## 💡 Actionable Recommendations
* **Contract Conversions:** Launch a targeted 10% discount campaign to transition high-risk Month-to-Month users to One-Year contracts.
* **Technical Audit:** Initiate an urgent technical and pricing review of the Fiber Optic service.
* **First 90-Days Care Program:** Overhaul the new customer onboarding experience with dedicated support to prevent immediate drop-off.
* **Auto-Pay Incentives:** Offer a one-time account credit to customers who switch from manual checks to automated payment setups.
